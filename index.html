Blink Eyes for macOS

Private reminders for healthier screen habits.

Blink Eyes is a local blink and screen-break reminder for Mac. Camera analysis stays on your device; the app has no account, advertising, analytics, or cloud service.

Get supportRead the privacy policy

On-device processing

Camera frames and eye landmarks are processed in memory on the Mac and are not uploaded.

Your control

Monitoring can be stopped at any time and the selected camera can be changed in Settings.

Wellness, not medical care

Blink Eyes supports screen-break habits. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions.
