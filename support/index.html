Support

Help with Blink Eyes.

Blink Eyes runs locally on your Mac. Start with the quick checks below, then contact support if you need help.

Email support

When writing to support, include only information you choose to share. Do not send camera frames or facial images unless essential. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Quick checks

The camera does not start

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and allow Blink Eyes. Then quit and reopen the app.

The wrong camera is active

Open the Blink Eyes menu bar item, choose Open Settings, refresh the available cameras, select the desired camera by name, and restart monitoring.

The blink counter is not updating

Check that monitoring is active and your face is visible to the selected camera. Open Show camera recognition to verify local landmark detection. If the status shows an error, include the displayed error text in your support email.

The reminder appears too early or too late

Open Settings and adjust Reminder after, sensitivity, and the centered reminder message. Sensitivity is personal: test changes while using your normal screen position and lighting.

What to include in a support request

  • Blink Eyes version and macOS version.
  • Your Mac model and the camera name selected in Settings.
  • A short description of the behavior and the steps that reproduce it.
  • The error text shown in the app, if any.

You do not need to share camera recordings, facial images, or full local error logs for a first support request.

Medical information

Blink Eyes is a personal wellness reminder, not medical software. For persistent eye symptoms or medical decisions, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
