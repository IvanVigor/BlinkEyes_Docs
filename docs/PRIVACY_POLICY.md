# Blink Eyes Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 5 August 2026
**Publisher:** Blink Eyes
**Privacy contact:** [obsidian.product.ai@gmail.com](mailto:obsidian.product.ai@gmail.com)

The public version is available at `/privacy/`. It describes the Blink Eyes macOS application, which is a wellness reminder and not a medical device.

## Local-only operation

Blink Eyes does not create an account and does not use advertising, analytics, telemetry, cloud storage, remote APIs, paid APIs, OpenAI services, or third-party network services. The app does not make network requests.

## Camera and blink analysis

The app requests Camera permission only to detect blink-related eye landmarks and show the optional local recognition preview. Camera frames, facial landmarks, and blink analysis are processed in memory on the user's Mac. They are not recorded, uploaded, transmitted, sold, shared, or retained after processing.

## Information stored locally

Blink Eyes stores only technical preferences in its local app container: selected camera name or identifier, reminder settings, target range, sensitivity, partial-blink option, and fade options. A local error log may contain technical exception text for troubleshooting. The app never stores camera frames, screenshots, facial landmarks, or a personal profile.

## Support emails

Support is optional. If you choose to email the support contact, your email address, message, and anything you voluntarily include are handled by the email providers involved in sending and receiving the message. This is outside the app's local processing flow: Blink Eyes does not send support emails or attach camera data automatically. You may request deletion of a support conversation by using the same email address.

## Sharing, tracking, and sales

Blink Eyes does not share app data with third parties, track people across apps or websites, or use IDFA, cookies, fingerprinting, marketing SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services.

## Changes and disclaimer

If a future version changes the local-only data model, this policy and the corresponding App Store privacy details will be updated before release. Blink Eyes does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions or persistent eye symptoms.
