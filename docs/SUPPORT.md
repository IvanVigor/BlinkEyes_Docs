# Blink Eyes Support

**Contact:** [obsidian.product.ai@gmail.com](mailto:obsidian.product.ai@gmail.com)

Blink Eyes processes camera input locally on the Mac. For support, include the app version, macOS version, selected camera name, a short reproduction path, and the error text shown in the app if present.

Do not send camera frames, facial images, or full local error logs unless they are essential and you have chosen to share them.

## Quick checks

- Grant Blink Eyes Camera permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera, then reopen the app.
- In Blink Eyes, open Settings, refresh cameras, select the desired camera by name, and restart monitoring.
- Use Show camera recognition to confirm eye landmarks are detected locally.
- Adjust Reminder after and sensitivity for your normal lighting and screen position.

Blink Eyes is a wellness reminder, not medical software. Seek qualified medical advice for persistent eye symptoms or medical decisions.
